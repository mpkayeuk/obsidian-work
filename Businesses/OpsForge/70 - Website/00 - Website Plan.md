# Website Plan

## Objectives
- Primary: Generate leads for high-value service engagements (Operating System Builds and Assurance).
- Secondary: Drive awareness and trials of the OpsForge SaaS platform and marketplace.
- Tertiary: Establish thought leadership and credibility in "practical AI for operations" and "building business operating systems."
- Support the hybrid model: Services as the visible entry point, platform as the scalable, self-serve future.

## Target Audiences & Messaging
1. **Agency / Consultancy Founders & Leaders** (primary service buyers)
   - Pain: Scaling delivery without chaos, inconsistent quality, onboarding friction.
   - Message: "Build your operating system once with expert help, then run and improve it with AI agents."
2. **Fractional Operators & Solo Service Providers**
   - Pain: Can't scale beyond personal capacity, knowledge not transferable.
   - Message: "Turn your expertise into systems that work without you in every step."
3. **Heads of Ops / Delivery in Growing Teams**
   - Pain: Process debt, tribal knowledge, tool sprawl without integration.
   - Message: "Structure what you already do, automate what should be automated, measure what matters."
4. **Curious AI / Ops Practitioners** (platform awareness)
   - Message: "Playbooks + agents + assurance. A better way to operationalize AI."

## Site Structure (Recommended Pages / Sections)
- **Home / Landing**
  - Hero with strong value prop + clear CTA (Book a discovery call / Start free trial / See how it works).
  - Problem statement (brief, relatable).
  - Solution overview (Capture → Structure → Deploy → Improve) with visuals.
  - Social proof / early case studies (or "How it works in practice").
  - Dual CTAs: Services (high-touch) and Platform (self-serve).
  - Trust signals (founder background, independence, expertise).

- **How It Works** (or Product)
  - Detailed flow with screenshots, short videos, or interactive demo if available.
  - Playbook examples (anonymized or template).
  - Agent capabilities and oversight model.
  - Platform features overview.
  - Link to pricing tiers.

- **Services**
  - Clear offering pages or sections: Operating System Build packages, Assurance Reviews, Custom Development, Workshops.
  - Process, deliverables, timelines, pricing guidance (or "starting at").
  - Case studies / outcomes (even if early/pilot).
  - Strong CTA to book a call or discovery workshop.

- **Platform / Product**
  - Self-serve focused: Features, benefits, who it's for.
  - Pricing table (clear comparison).
  - Marketplace teaser.
  - Sign-up / trial CTA.
  - Comparison or "why OpsForge vs. generic AI/automation tools."

- **Resources / Learn**
  - Blog or content hub (playbook templates, AI ops guides, case studies, "building your operating system" series).
  - Free resources (checklists, starter playbooks, webinars).
  - Link to any affiliated or related content (e.g., ops content on AI in operations).

- **About / Founder**
  - Credibility: Background in infrastructure, operations at scale, AI application.
  - Why OpsForge (personal motivation, lessons from prior work).
  - Team (if any) or "built with operators, for operators."
  - Contact / connect.

- **Pricing**
  - Transparent services packages + SaaS tiers.
  - FAQ around hybrid model, what you get, etc.
  - "Talk to us" for custom/enterprise.

- **Contact / Book**
  - Simple form for discovery calls.
  - Calendly embed for direct booking.
  - Email and LinkedIn.

- **Footer:** Legal (terms, privacy, disclaimers), social links, copyright.

## Design & Branding
- Professional, modern, trustworthy — clean typography, good use of space.
- Industrial/craft feel from "Forge" (structured, reliable, built with care) balanced with approachable and modern (AI-enabled).
- Colors: Blues/greens for trust and operations, with accent for energy/AI.
- Visuals: Real process examples, clean screenshots of playbooks/agents/dashboards, before/after operational clarity.
- Tone: Expert but not arrogant; practical and operator-focused; confident in the "playbooks that run" philosophy.
- Mobile-first experience (many operators work across devices).

## Technical Considerations
- Next.js (aligns with planned product stack) for performance, SEO, and easy iteration.
- Good SEO foundation (structured data, fast loads, clear headings).
- Analytics (Vercel + product analytics tie-in).
- Forms that feed CRM or email list (simple to start: email + Notion/Airtable or CRM).
- Accessibility and performance as table stakes.
- Easy content updates (MDX or headless CMS if volume grows).

## Content & SEO Priorities
- Target terms: "AI playbook builder", "business operating system", "agency operations AI", "process automation for service businesses", "playbooks and agents", "operational excellence AI".
- Lead magnets: Free playbook templates, "Operating System Audit" checklist, "AI in Ops" guides.
- Case studies: Even early ones with clear before/after (time saved, consistency improved, stress reduced).
- Blog cadence: 1-2 high-quality pieces per month initially.

## Conversion Optimization
- Multiple CTAs per page (services vs. platform).
- Clear value differentiation between services (speed, customization, expertise) and platform (ongoing access, lower cost, self-serve scale).
- Social proof, testimonials, and outcome-focused language.
- Low-friction entry (free resources, trial, small discovery workshop).

## Measurement
- Primary: Service inquiries / booked calls, platform sign-ups/trials.
- Secondary: Content engagement, time on site, email list growth, marketplace views.
- Tools: Analytics platform + simple CRM for lead tracking.

## Phased Approach
- **MVP Site (now/soon):** Core pages (Home, Services, How it Works, About, Contact). Strong service focus + platform teaser. Calendly + basic forms.
- **Post-MVP:** Add detailed pricing, more case studies, resources hub, marketplace section, self-serve signup flow.
- **Ongoing:** Iterate based on what converts (A/B test headlines/CTAs, add testimonials, expand content).

## Domain Strategy
- Primary: opsology.io (owned) — can serve as the main brand site or redirect to OpsForge experience.
- OpsForge as product/brand name with clear explanation on site (or sub-path /opsforge).
- Consider opsforge.com or other variants later if desired; opsology.io provides immediate credibility and "ops" thematic fit.

This plan keeps the site focused on generating the right kind of leads while supporting the long-term platform vision. It should feel professional and expert while remaining approachable for operators who want practical help, not more complexity.
