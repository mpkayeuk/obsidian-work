# Service Offerings Overview

OpsForge combines high-touch expert services with a self-serve SaaS platform and marketplace. Services provide rapid value, deep customization, case studies, and a natural on-ramp to the platform.

## Core Philosophy
Services are not a permanent dependency. They are an accelerator: we help you build your initial operating system quickly and correctly, transfer knowledge, and then equip you to own, extend, and improve it yourself via the platform.

## Main Service Lines

### 1. Operating System Build (Core Engagement)
Rapid capture and deployment of a client's foundational operating system.

**What's Included:**
- Discovery workshop(s) to identify highest-leverage processes.
- AI-assisted + expert conversational capture of processes into structured playbooks.
- Refinement, validation, and approval of playbooks (including edge cases, decision points, required evidence).
- Deployment of initial AI agents with integrations to key tools (Slack, email, calendars, CRM, docs, etc.).
- Training and enablement for the team.
- Initial observability setup (logs, metrics, alerts).
- Optional: Basic marketplace template preparation.

**Deliverables:**
- 8-15 production-ready playbooks.
- Deployed agents with runbooks and oversight.
- Dashboard and reporting foundation.
- Documentation and knowledge transfer.
- 30-60 day support window.

**Pricing Model:** Fixed-scope packages or time-boxed (e.g., 4-8 week engagements). £5,000 – £18,000+ based on number of playbooks, complexity, and integrations.

**Target Outcome:** Client has a working core operating system delivering immediate consistency and time savings, plus the skills and platform access to expand it.

### 2. Premium Assurance & Review
Leverages the independent infrastructure/technical assurance heritage.

**Offerings:**
- One-time or periodic "Operating System Health Check" — expert review of playbooks, agent performance, risks, and improvement opportunities.
- Ongoing Assurance Retainer — scheduled reviews + on-call advisory for critical processes.
- Pre-Deployment Validation — expert sign-off before rolling out new high-stakes playbooks/agents.

**Pricing:** £1,500 – £7,500 per review cycle or £1,000 – £3,000/month retainer.

**Value:** Provides the "cannot afford failure" rigor for important processes while the platform handles day-to-day execution.

### 3. Custom Agent & Playbook Development
For complex or highly specific needs beyond standard templates.

- Bespoke agent development (multi-step workflows, custom tool integrations, advanced logic).
- Industry or function-specific playbook packs (e.g., client delivery for agencies, project kickoff, reporting automation).
- Integration development or custom connectors.

Priced as add-ons to builds or standalone projects.

### 4. Training, Workshops & Enablement
- "Build Your Own Playbooks" workshops (founder + team training).
- Change management and adoption support during rollout.
- OpsForge certification programs (for internal champions or external consultants).

These services create additional revenue and increase platform stickiness.

## Service-to-Platform Flywheel
1. Client engages for a build service.
2. We deliver high value quickly using the platform + expert facilitation.
3. Client sees the power of structured playbooks + agents.
4. Natural conversion to ongoing SaaS subscription for maintenance, expansion, analytics, and new agents.
5. Client may publish successful playbooks to the marketplace (additional revenue share opportunity).
6. Success stories and templates feed future services and platform adoption.

## Delivery Model
- Hybrid human + AI: Experts guide the process and provide judgment; AI handles capture, drafting, consistency checking, and initial agent generation.
- Remote-first with optional on-site workshops for larger engagements.
- Clear scope, deliverables, and success criteria defined upfront.
- Post-engagement support included to ensure smooth handover to self-serve.

## Future Evolution
As the platform matures, services will become more efficient (more templated, faster AI assistance) while maintaining the high-touch customization and assurance that clients value. This improves margins and capacity over time.

Services are the commercial engine in the early phase and a key differentiator long-term.
