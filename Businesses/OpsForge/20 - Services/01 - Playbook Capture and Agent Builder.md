# Playbook Capture and Agent Builder (Service Offering)

## Description
A focused service to rapidly extract, structure, and operationalize a client's key processes into production-ready playbooks and initial AI agents. This is often the entry point or core of a larger Operating System Build engagement.

## Target Use Cases
- Client onboarding / delivery processes.
- Proposal and sales handoff.
- Project kickoffs and status reporting.
- Quality assurance and review workflows.
- Knowledge handoff and documentation.
- Recurring operational routines (billing, compliance checks, client communications, etc.).

## What the Service Delivers
1. **Process Discovery & Prioritization**
   - Collaborative workshop to map the client's current state, pain points, and highest-ROI processes.
   - Identification of 5-10 (or more) priority processes for the initial build.

2. **Conversational + AI-Assisted Capture**
   - Structured interviews (human + AI) with the people who actually perform the work.
   - Import and analysis of existing artifacts (docs, emails, Slack threads, Notion pages, etc.).
   - AI drafts initial playbook structures; expert refines for accuracy, completeness, and edge cases.

3. **Playbook Structuring & Validation**
   - Playbooks formatted with clear steps, decision logic, inputs/outputs, success criteria, required evidence/approvals, and escalation paths.
   - Version control, audit trail, and approval workflow.
   - Testing of logic with real or sample scenarios.
   - Client review and sign-off.

4. **Agent Deployment & Integration**
   - Configuration of AI agents to execute or guide the playbook (notifications, data entry, document generation, tool actions where safe and approved).
   - Connections to client's existing tools (Slack, Google Workspace, email, CRM, calendars, project tools, etc.).
   - Human-in-the-loop checkpoints for judgment calls or high-stakes actions.
   - Logging, observability, and basic analytics from day one.

5. **Handover & Enablement**
   - Team training on using and maintaining playbooks/agents.
   - Documentation and runbooks.
   - 30-day post-launch support for tweaks and questions.
   - Guidance on next steps for expansion via self-serve platform.

## Deliverables
- Structured, versioned playbooks (exportable).
- Deployed and tested AI agents.
- Integration configurations and credentials setup.
- Usage dashboard access (during and after engagement).
- Training materials and recorded sessions.
- Recommendations report for further operating system development.

## Pricing Guidance
- Standalone Playbook + Agent Pack (5-8 processes): £4,000 – £8,000.
- Larger scoped as part of full Operating System Build.
- Add-ons: Additional processes, complex custom integrations, premium assurance review.

## Differentiation in Delivery
- Grounded in real operational experience (not generic AI prompting).
- Emphasis on auditability, reliability, and human oversight points.
- Structured output (playbooks) rather than one-off automations.
- Designed for handover — client owns the intellectual property and can evolve it independently.

## Success Metrics (for Client)
- Time saved on target processes.
- Reduction in errors or rework.
- Faster onboarding or delivery cycles.
- Increased consistency and visibility.
- Team confidence in the new system.
