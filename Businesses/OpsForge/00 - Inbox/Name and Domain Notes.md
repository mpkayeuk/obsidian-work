# Name and Domain Notes

## Current Working Name
**OpsForge**

- Evokes "forging" a strong, reliable operating system from raw processes and expertise.
- "Ops" directly signals operations and practical business running.
- Industrial/craftsmanship feel aligns with building durable systems (playbooks + agents) rather than disposable automations.
- Short, memorable, ownable in most contexts.

## Domain Situation
- Primary owned asset: **opsology.io**
  - Strong thematic fit ("the study / science / practice of operations").
  - Professional .io extension suitable for tech/SaaS/ops tools.
  - Provides immediate credibility and avoids the need to hunt for new domains in early stages.
- OpsForge.com and close variants appear taken or premium (common for "Forge" names in tech/ops spaces).
- Strategy options:
  1. Use opsology.io as the main company/brand domain, with OpsForge as the product name or experience (e.g., "OpsForge by Opsology" or clear branding on site).
  2. Treat OpsForge as internal/working name and evolve public brand around Opsology (or Opsology Forge / Opsology OS).
  3. Monitor and pursue aftermarket for ideal OpsForge variant if budget allows later.
  4. Secure common alternatives (opsforge.io, opsforge.app, etc.) as defensive registrations if cheap.

## Branding Considerations
- **Pros of OpsForge:** Distinct, action-oriented, differentiates from pure "Opsology" assurance service positioning. Good for the AI platform/product side.
- **Pros of leaning on Opsology:** Leverages owned domain, existing (or related) credibility in infrastructure/ops assurance, "ology" implies systematic expertise and methodology — excellent for playbooks and operating systems.
- Recommendation (high-level): Launch with opsology.io as the home, prominently feature "OpsForge" as the AI operating system platform/product. This gives the best of both (domain asset + strong product name). Over time, decide whether to fully brand under one or keep the dual feel.

## Other Name Ideas Considered (for reference)
- ForgeOS / SysForge / OpsCraft / PlayForge / ForgeCore / OpsSmith (many have conflicts or availability issues).
- Opsology variants or sub-brands (e.g., Opsology OS, Opsology Forge).
