# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Phase 1: Foundation & Validation (Months 1-4)
**Goal:** Prove demand, generate initial revenue via services, build core platform MVP, and create referenceable outcomes.

**Tactics:**
- **Content & Thought Leadership:** Publish consistently on practical AI for operations, playbook design, scaling service businesses, and "building your operating system." Use LinkedIn, personal networks, and owned channels. Leverage any existing audience from prior work (CIOJoy, 4mie, consulting).
- **Direct Outreach & Conversations:** 20-30 targeted conversations with agency owners, fractional operators, and service business leaders. Offer paid pilot/discovery workshops.
- **Service-Led Acquisition:** Sell 3-8 Operating System Build engagements at premium prices. Deliver exceptional results. Use these as case studies and templates.
- **Community & Partnerships:** Engage in relevant Slack/Discord communities, agency networks, fractional operator groups, and ops-focused events. Offer affiliate or referral arrangements.
- **MVP Platform:** Build enough of the capture → playbook → basic agent flow to support service delivery and give clients self-serve access post-engagement.

**Metrics:** 3-8 service clients, positive case studies, initial platform usage data, clear product feedback loops.

**Positioning:** "Expert-built operating systems with AI agents — start with hands-on help, own it forever."

## Phase 2: Platform Traction & Flywheel (Months 4-9)
**Goal:** Convert service clients to recurring SaaS, launch marketplace, drive organic growth, and increase inbound.

**Tactics:**
- **Service-to-SaaS Conversion:** Structured handover and onboarding from every service engagement into platform subscriptions. Offer launch discounts or included months.
- **Content Engine:** Double down on high-value content (playbook templates, agent examples, "before/after" operating system case studies, AI ops playbooks). SEO for terms like "AI playbook builder," "business operating system," "agency operations AI."
- **Marketplace Launch:** Seed with high-quality playbooks from early clients and founder expertise. Promote user-generated content.
- **Partnerships:** Formalize relationships with fractional networks, agency coaches, and complementary tool providers (Notion templates, automation tools, CRM platforms). Co-marketing and referral programs.
- **Community Building:** OpsForge user community (Slack/Discord) for sharing playbooks, agents, and wins. Host regular "Ops Office Hours" or webinars.
- **Light Paid Acquisition:** Test LinkedIn and targeted content ads once case studies exist. Focus on retargeting and lookalikes from service clients.

**Metrics:** Service-to-SaaS conversion rate >60%, growing MRR, first marketplace transactions, organic inbound leads, active user community.

## Phase 3: Scale & Expansion (Month 9+)
**Goal:** Product-led growth, broader awareness, vertical expansion, and team leverage.

**Tactics:**
- **Product-Led Elements:** Free tier or low-barrier entry (limited agents/playbooks) to allow self-serve trials and viral/template sharing.
- **Vertical Packs:** Develop or curate industry-specific playbook/agent packs (e.g., digital agencies, management consultancies, coaching practices) for faster sales.
- **Thought Leadership at Scale:** Expand to podcasts, conferences, guest posts, and potentially a more formal content site or newsletter.
- **Sales Enablement:** For larger deals, introduce light sales support (demos, ROI calculators, pilot programs).
- **Partnership Ecosystem:** White-label or reseller options for consultants and agencies who want to offer OpsForge to their clients.
- **International:** Expand content and partnerships in additional English-speaking markets; consider localization later.

**Metrics:** Self-serve signups as % of growth, marketplace revenue contribution, expansion MRR (upgrades, additional agents), brand search volume, partnership-sourced revenue.

## Channel Mix Evolution
- **Early:** 80%+ service delivery + direct relationships + content.
- **Mid:** Balanced between services, content/SEO, partnerships, and platform product.
- **Later:** Heavier on product-led growth, marketplace, and partnerships, with services as high-margin upsell or enterprise entry.

## Key Messages by Stage
- **Awareness:** "Stop fighting process chaos as you grow."
- **Consideration:** "Turn your expertise into a reliable operating system with AI agents."
- **Decision:** "Start fast with expert help, then own and scale it yourself."
- **Retention/Expansion:** "Your operating system gets smarter and more valuable every month."

## Risks & Mitigations
- Slow service pipeline → Aggressive content and outreach; offer smaller entry workshops.
- Platform not ready for self-serve → Keep services as primary engine longer; use platform internally to support delivery.
- Low marketplace adoption → Heavy seeding with high-quality content from services; incentives for early publishers.
- Brand confusion with existing "ops" tools → Clear positioning around "playbooks + agents + assurance" and operating system concept.

This GTM is designed to be capital-efficient, leverage the founder's strengths in services and operations, and build a durable platform business over time.
