# Target Market

## Primary Market
**Service businesses and knowledge-work teams that deliver value through processes, expertise, and client interactions.**

### Core Segments
1. **Agencies & Consultancies** (digital, marketing, strategy, design, HR, etc.)
   - Pain: Inconsistent delivery, slow onboarding of new team members or clients, knowledge silos, proposal-to-delivery handoff friction.
   - Size: Small to mid-sized (5-150 people) that are growing or feel operational strain.

2. **Fractional Operators & Service Providers**
   - Solo or small teams offering fractional CFO/COO/CMO, project management, operations support, or specialized services.
   - Pain: Scaling beyond one person, documenting "how we do things," maintaining quality while taking on more clients.
   - High willingness to invest in systems that create leverage.

3. **Professional Services Firms & Boutiques**
   - Legal, accounting, advisory, coaching, training, and other expertise-driven practices.
   - Pain: Variable quality, knowledge management, client experience consistency, scaling without diluting expertise.

4. **Internal Ops / Delivery Teams in Tech & Product Companies**
   - Teams responsible for customer success, implementation, support processes, or internal operations that have grown complex.
   - Secondary but valuable for platform adoption.

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Growing or recently grown (hitting scaling pains).
- Already using or experimenting with AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, automation) but struggling to turn experiments into reliable systems.
- Leadership that values operational discipline and sees process as a competitive advantage.
- Decision makers: Founders, Heads of Operations/Delivery, Managing Directors, or fractional operators themselves.
- Budget: Can invest £5k+ in services or £100-1k+/mo in SaaS when ROI is clear (time saved, quality, capacity).

## Secondary / Expansion Markets
- Startups moving from founder-led to team-delivered operations.
- Non-profits or mission-driven organizations with complex stakeholder processes.
- Larger enterprises with specific "center of excellence" or transformation initiatives (later stage).

## Geographic Focus (Initial)
- UK (strong service economy, founder location advantages).
- US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand (English-speaking, similar business models).
- Later: EU and other English-primary markets.

## Why This Market First?
- Acute pain with process and knowledge management.
- High value placed on reliability and client experience.
- Natural fit for playbooks (repeatable but judgment-heavy work).
- Hybrid model resonates (many already buy fractional/consulting services).
- Lower regulatory barriers than heavily compliance-driven verticals.
- Clear path to marketplace (industry-specific packs for agencies, consultancies, etc.).

## Market Sizing Notes (High-Level)
- Hundreds of thousands of agencies and professional service firms in target geographies.
- Millions of individual operators and team members experiencing these pains.
- AI adoption in operations is accelerating, creating urgency and openness to better tools.
- Average deal size supports healthy unit economics (services £5-15k; SaaS £200-2k+/mo for teams).

## Go-to-Market Implications
- Content and thought leadership around "building your operating system," practical AI for ops, and scaling service delivery without chaos.
- Case studies and templates that speak directly to agency/consultancy realities.
- Partnerships with fractional networks, agency communities, and ops-focused creators.
- Service offerings as the primary acquisition engine initially, with platform as the retention and expansion vehicle.

This market is large enough for meaningful scale yet focused enough for strong product-market fit and differentiation.
