# Business Model

## Overview
OpsForge uses a **hybrid service + SaaS + marketplace** model. This allows fast initial revenue and high customization through services while building a scalable, recurring-revenue product business.

The model is designed to:
- Deliver quick, high-value outcomes (services).
- Create a compounding platform asset (SaaS).
- Generate network effects and additional revenue (marketplace).
- Maintain high margins and customer control.

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Services (High-Margin, Fast Revenue)
- **Core Operating System Build:** Fixed or time-boxed engagements to capture, structure, and deploy the client's first 8-15 core playbooks + agents. Includes workshops, AI-assisted capture, validation, initial integrations, and training.
  - Typical price: £4,000 – £15,000+ depending on scope and complexity.
- **Premium Assurance & Review:** Senior expert review, ongoing monitoring, or "OpsForge Assurance" add-on (ties to infrastructure assurance heritage).
  - Price: £1,500 – £6,000 per review cycle or monthly retainer.
- **Custom Agent Development & Training:** Bespoke agents or vertical playbook packs.
- **Implementation & Change Management Support:** Workshops and ongoing support during rollout.

Services act as the wedge: they generate immediate cash, validate the platform, produce case studies and templates, and often convert into long-term SaaS customers.

### 2. SaaS Subscriptions (Recurring, Scalable)
Tiered access to the OpsForge platform for playbook management, agent deployment, execution logs, analytics, and basic marketplace access.

- **Starter / Essentials** (£99–£199/mo): Limited agents/playbooks, basic capture, core integrations, individual use.
- **Growth / Team** (£399–£799/mo): More concurrent agents, team collaboration, advanced analytics, priority support, marketplace publishing rights.
- **Scale / Enterprise** (£1,500+/mo or custom): Unlimited agents/playbooks (within fair use), SSO, advanced security/audit, custom integrations, dedicated support, white-label options, SLA.

Usage-based add-ons: Additional agents, heavy execution volume, premium AI models.

Billing: Monthly or annual (discount for annual). Easy upgrade path from services customers.

### 3. Marketplace (Network Effects + Transaction Revenue)
- Users and approved experts can publish playbooks and agents to the marketplace.
- Revenue share: 70/30 or 80/20 (creator/OpsForge) on paid templates/agents.
- Free tier for basic discovery and community playbooks to drive adoption.
- Potential for OpsForge-curated "verified" or "OpsForge Certified" packs with higher trust/revenue share.

This creates a flywheel: more users → more templates → faster value for new users → more users.

### 4. Future / Adjacent
- White-label or embedded versions for agencies/consultancies (B2B SaaS).
- Training and certification programs.
- Data/insights products (anonymized operational benchmarks).

## Unit Economics (High-Level Assumptions)
- Services gross margin: 65-80% (expert time + AI costs).
- SaaS gross margin: 75-85% (after AI inference, hosting, support).
- Marketplace take rate: 20-30%.
- Blended LTV high due to services-to-SaaS conversion and recurring + marketplace.
- CAC: Content marketing + service delivery reputation + referrals initially; paid channels later.
- Payback: Services pay back quickly; SaaS within 3-6 months.

## Go-to-Market Alignment
Services provide the initial beachhead and proof. Platform usage during services creates sticky product adoption. Marketplace accelerates organic growth.

## Pricing Philosophy
- Transparent and value-based.
- Easy entry via smaller services or Starter tier.
- Clear upgrade path as the operating system grows in scope and importance.
- Fair usage with transparent overage.

This model balances the speed-to-revenue of services with the scalability of SaaS while adding marketplace leverage — a stronger combination than pure services or pure product plays.
