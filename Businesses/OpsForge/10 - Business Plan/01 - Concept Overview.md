# Concept Overview

## The Problem
Most service businesses and growing teams run on tribal knowledge, scattered docs, email threads, and heroic individual effort. As they scale:

- Processes are inconsistent or undocumented.
- Knowledge walks out the door when people leave.
- Repetitive work consumes high-value time.
- Scaling creates chaos rather than leverage.
- AI tools are adopted piecemeal but rarely integrated into reliable operational systems.

The result: high stress, variable quality, slow onboarding, and a ceiling on growth without proportional headcount increases.

## The Solution: OpsForge
OpsForge is an AI-native platform that helps businesses systematically capture, structure, automate, and improve their operations.

**Core Loop:**
1. **Capture** — Conversational AI interviews (or import from docs/emails) to extract processes into structured playbooks.
2. **Structure** — Playbooks are versioned, auditable documents with steps, decision trees, inputs/outputs, success criteria, and required evidence.
3. **Deploy** — One-click (or guided) deployment as AI agents that can act in tools (email, Slack, CRM, calendars, docs, etc.) or guide humans.
4. **Run & Improve** — Agents execute or assist, log everything, surface metrics, suggest improvements, and allow human override/feedback.
5. **Scale** — Marketplace for industry/playbook templates; team collaboration; expert assurance add-ons.

**Hybrid Delivery Model (Key Differentiator):**
- **Services Layer (Fast Value, High Margin):** Senior operators + AI specialists rapidly build a client's core operating system (first 5-15 playbooks + agents) in weeks. Includes workshops, validation, and initial assurance.
- **Platform Layer (Recurring, Scalable):** Self-serve SaaS for ongoing use, updates, new agents, analytics, and marketplace access. Subscription tiers based on number of active agents/playbooks and team size.
- **Marketplace:** Users and experts can publish, sell, or license playbooks/agents. OpsForge takes a revenue share.
- **Assurance Add-on:** Optional senior expert review and ongoing monitoring (ties back to original Opsology positioning for trust).

This combines the speed and customization of services with the leverage and recurring revenue of SaaS.

## Target Scope (MVP Focus)
- Service businesses and knowledge work (agencies, consultancies, fractional operators, professional services, ops-heavy teams).
- Processes that are repeatable but currently manual or inconsistent (client onboarding, reporting, proposal generation, project kickoffs, quality reviews, knowledge handoff, etc.).
- Integration with common tools (Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, CRMs, calendars, email).

## Differentiation
- **Playbooks First:** Not generic agents or chatbots. Structured, auditable playbooks are the source of truth.
- **Hybrid Path:** Service-to-product flywheel (services fund and validate the platform; platform makes services more efficient and scalable).
- **Assurance Heritage:** Grounded in real operational expertise and risk awareness (independent review DNA).
- **Execution + Improvement:** Closed loop of run, measure, refine — not just one-time automation.
- **Marketplace Leverage:** Network effects from shared playbooks accelerate value for all users.

## High-Level Roadmap
- Phase 1 (Validation/MVP): Core capture → playbook → basic agent deployment. Hybrid service offering.
- Phase 2: Full agent actions in tools, analytics, team features, initial marketplace.
- Phase 3: Advanced AI (multi-agent workflows, auto-improvement suggestions), broader integrations, expert assurance productized layer.
- Phase 4: Vertical packs, white-label for consultants/agencies, international/expanded templates.

The name "OpsForge" evokes crafting reliable operating systems (forge = build with strength and precision). Primary domain strategy includes the owned opsology.io (with OpsForge as product/brand name or sub-experience).
