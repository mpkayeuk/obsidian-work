# Pricing and Subscription Tiers

## Pricing Philosophy
Transparent, value-based pricing that supports the hybrid model. Services deliver fast, high-value outcomes and often convert into platform subscriptions. SaaS provides ongoing access, leverage, and improvement at a predictable cost. Marketplace adds upside for creators and the platform.

All prices in GBP (adjust for other markets). Annual billing offers 15-20% discount.

## Services Pricing (High-Touch, Project-Based)

### Operating System Build Packages
- **Foundation Pack** (Core 5-8 playbooks + agents): £5,000 – £8,000
  - Discovery workshop, capture, structuring, basic integrations, training, 30-day support.
- **Growth Pack** (10-15 playbooks + agents): £9,000 – £15,000
  - Everything in Foundation + advanced workflows, team collaboration setup, analytics foundation, marketplace readiness.
- **Enterprise / Custom Build**: £15,000+
  - Larger scope, complex integrations, custom agents, multi-team, ongoing support options.

Add-ons:
- Premium Assurance Review: +£2,000 – £5,000
- Additional playbooks/agents: £600 – £1,200 each
- Custom integrations or advanced training: Quoted separately

**Engagement Terms:** 50% upfront, 50% on delivery/handover. Clear scope and deliverables in Statement of Work. 30-60 day post-engagement support window included.

### Standalone Offerings
- One-time Playbook + Agent Pack (smaller scope): £3,500 – £6,000
- Operating System Health Check / Assurance Review: £1,500 – £4,500
- Custom Agent Development (per complex workflow): £1,500 – £5,000+
- Workshops / Training (half or full day): £1,000 – £2,500

## SaaS Subscription Tiers (Platform Access)

### Essentials (Starter)
- **Price:** £99 / month or £950 / year
- **For:** Solo operators, very small teams, or early testing.
- **Limits:** Up to 5 active playbooks, 3 concurrent agents, basic capture tools, core integrations (Slack, email, Google Workspace, calendar), execution logs, basic analytics.
- **Features:** Playbook editor, agent deployment (with human approval defaults), export (PDF/Markdown/JSON), community marketplace access (read).
- **Support:** Email/community.

### Growth (Team)
- **Price:** £399 / month or £3,800 / year
- **For:** Growing teams and small agencies that need real operational leverage.
- **Limits:** Up to 25 active playbooks, 15 concurrent agents, team workspaces, advanced analytics and reporting, priority AI models, marketplace publishing (standard).
- **Features:** Everything in Essentials + collaboration (comments, approvals, roles), advanced agent actions (more tool integrations), version history & rollback, performance dashboards, basic templates, audit exports.
- **Support:** Email + priority response. Onboarding call.

### Scale (Business / Enterprise)
- **Price:** £1,499 / month or £14,400 / year (custom pricing available for larger or complex needs)
- **For:** Larger agencies, professional services firms, or organizations running significant portions of their operations through OpsForge.
- **Limits:** Higher or custom limits on playbooks/agents, usage-based overages available, SSO/SAML, advanced security & compliance features (SOC2 path), dedicated support.
- **Features:** Everything in Growth + white-label options (limited), advanced multi-agent workflows, custom integrations or private marketplace, SLA, usage analytics & optimization recommendations, expert assurance credits or priority scheduling.
- **Support:** Dedicated account manager or priority Slack/ email. Quarterly business reviews optional.

### Add-Ons (Across Tiers)
- Additional agents/playbooks (beyond tier limit): £25–£60 per item / month.
- Premium AI usage / higher model access: Usage-based.
- Marketplace revenue share for publishers: 70-80% to creator (platform takes 20-30%).
- Expert Assurance credits or add-on reviews: Separate or bundled.
- White-label / embedded (Scale tier): Custom quote.

## Marketplace Economics
- Free discovery and basic templates.
- Paid templates/agents: Creator sets price; OpsForge takes 20-30% platform fee.
- Verified / OpsForge Certified packs: Higher visibility and potentially different revenue share.
- Goal: Accelerate value for buyers while rewarding high-quality creators (including power users and partners).

## Payment & Billing
- Stripe for all transactions (cards, invoices, ACH where available).
- Services: Invoicing with milestone payments.
- SaaS: Recurring subscriptions with easy upgrades/downgrades. Prorated changes.
- Marketplace: Payouts to creators on a schedule (net 30 or similar).
- Taxes: Handled via Stripe Tax; customer responsible for local taxes where applicable.

## Free / Trial Options
- Limited free tier or 14-30 day trial of Growth features (with usage caps) to allow self-serve evaluation.
- During service engagements: Full platform access included for the duration + handover period.
- Community / read-only marketplace access always free.

## Positioning Notes
- Services priced to feel like a worthwhile investment with clear ROI (time saved, capacity gained, risk reduced).
- SaaS priced competitively with other operational/AI tools but justified by the structured playbook + agent + assurance combination.
- Clear upgrade path from services customer → Essentials → Growth → Scale.
- Annual discounts encourage commitment and improve cash flow.

## Future Adjustments
Pricing will be reviewed based on customer feedback, value delivered (time saved metrics), competitive landscape, and AI cost trends. The model is designed to be flexible as the product matures and marketplace revenue grows.

All pricing and terms subject to formal quotes, Statements of Work, and Terms of Service. Early customers may receive grandfathered or custom terms.
