## Slide 2: The Problem
- Growing service businesses and knowledge teams hit an operational ceiling.
- Tribal knowledge, inconsistent execution, slow onboarding, process chaos during scale.
- AI tools are everywhere but rarely create reliable, auditable systems.
- Result: Stress, variable quality, growth limited by headcount rather than leverage, knowledge loss when people leave.
- "We have tools and people, but no operating system."

## Slide 3: The Solution
OpsForge is the AI platform that turns your processes into a custom, executable operating system:
- Capture real expertise as structured, auditable playbooks.
- Deploy as reliable AI agents with human oversight where it matters.
- Run, measure, and continuously improve.
- Hybrid entry: Expert services for fast results + self-serve SaaS + marketplace for scale.

One operating system. Built from your playbooks. Powered by AI. Owned by you.
