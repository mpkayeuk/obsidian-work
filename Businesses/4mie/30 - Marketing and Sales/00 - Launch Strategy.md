# Marketing & Sales Strategy

## Launch Plan
1. **Pre-Launch**: SEO content ('Form E forensic audit', 'common Form E mistakes', 'surviving financial disclosure'), Reddit r/Divorce_UK posts focusing on credibility.
2. **Channels**: Google Ads (£1k/mo 'form e help', 'financial disclosure audit'), Facebook (divorce groups), TikTok stories showing "Red Team" audit in action.
3. **Content**: Free "Form E Credibility Scorecard" or "Common Math Errors Checklist" (lead mag).
4. **Partners**: Affiliate with divorce coaches, mediators (referring clients who need their disclosure audited).

## Sales Funnel
1. **Free Initial Audit**: User enters basic income/expense figures → AI flags 1-2 major inconsistencies for free.
2. **Upsell Pro**: Full Form E generation + the complete **Forensic Audit Shield** to fix all errors.
3. **Solicitor Refer**: Upsell human review for final sign-off.

## Metrics
CAC < £50, LTV £250+, Conversion 5% visitors.

Budget Y1: £24k.
