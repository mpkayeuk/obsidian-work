# Idea: Pay for Divorce Settlement Results

## Core Idea

Pay customers for the final results of their divorce final settlement. This provides a strong incentive for users to see the process through with the app and to provide valuable data.

## Feedback Loop and App Improvement

*   **Survey Each Customer:** Implement a system to survey each customer at the conclusion of their settlement process.
*   **Timeline-Based Prompting:** Use a timeline collected within the app to prompt the user for feedback on each specific step of the settlement process.
*   **Data Analysis:** Analyse the collected data to identify:
    *   Common pain points and bottlenecks in the divorce process.
    *   Areas where the app is most and least effective.
    *   Opportunities for new features and improvements.
*   **Positive Feedback Loop:** The insights gained from this data will create a positive feedback loop, allowing for continuous improvement of the app and better outcomes for future users.
