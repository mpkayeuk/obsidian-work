## Key Frameworks from the Video

### 1. Medium & Message Framework

To stand out, you need BOTH:

**High Signal Medium** (Y-axis):
- Rarely used channel
- Less noisy
- More likely to be opened
- More direct
- Emotionally disarming

**High Impact Message** (X-axis):
- Emotionally resonant
- Personalised
- Shows you understand them
- Memorable

| | Low Impact | High Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **High Signal** | Wasted opportunity | ✅ **WIN** |
| **Low Signal (Noisy)** | Invisible | Still lost in noise |

**Example**: Harry got the job by sending a 7-minute personalised video (high impact) via direct LinkedIn messages to executives (high signal), not through the recruitment inbox (noisy).

---

### 2. RICE Framework - The Four Motivations

When crafting any message, speak to one of these:

| Letter | Motivation | Description | Example |
|--------|------------|-------------|---------|
| **R** | Reward | Money, benefits, recognition | "Save £2,000 on solicitor fees" |
| **I** | Ideology | Beliefs, values, causes | "Everyone deserves fair divorce disclosure" |
| **C** | Coercion | Fear, guilt, consequences | "Errors on Form E can result in penalties" |
| **E** | Ego | How they see themselves | "Take control of your divorce" |

**Key insight**: Understand what motivates your audience, then connect that to what you want them to do.

---

### 3. The 90-Day Content Challenge

**The commitment**:
1. Pick ONE platform
2. Post ONCE per day
3. For 90 days straight (including weekends)
4. No excuses, no "when the time is right"

**Why this works**:

1. **Kills the debate** - You only have to win the internal argument once (today), not every day
2. **Compounding returns begin** - Slow then fast
3. **You learn 600% faster** than someone posting weekly

**The math** (assuming 0.5% growth per post):
- Person A (weekly): 12,000 followers after 10 years
- Person B (daily): 1,000,000+ followers after 10 years
- That's 8,500% more followers

---

### 4. The Three Ps

Every post follows this cycle:

1. **Produce** - Create the content
2. **Publish** - Put it out there
3. **Pay Attention** - Study the data (views, clicks, drop-off, comments)

Then repeat. Each lap teaches you something.

> "Every single post is data."

---

### 5. Personas

Steven's team read thousands of comments and created **4 personas** that represent their audience. Every second of content must serve at least one persona.

Their personas range from:
- CEO of a $100B company (they know by name)
- Someone in a normal job with dreams and ideas
- Everything in between

## Applying to 4mie

### 4mie's Target Personas

Based on the LIP market research, here are 4 personas for 4mie content:

#### Persona 1: "Sarah" - The Overwhelmed Professional
- **Who**: 35-45, employed, decent income but can't afford £5k+ for solicitor
- **Situation**: Amicable-ish divorce, wants to get it done properly without drama
- **Pain**: Form E is confusing, doesn't know what to disclose, worried about mistakes
- **Motivation**: **Reward** (save money) + **Ego** (handle this professionally)
- **Content that serves her**: "How to value your pension for Form E", "5 mistakes that get Form E rejected"

#### Persona 2: "James" - The Contested Fighter
- **Who**: 40-55, mid-conflict divorce, spouse being difficult
- **Situation**: Needs to protect himself, worried about spouse hiding assets
- **Pain**: Feels powerless against spouse's solicitor, can't afford equal firepower
- **Motivation**: **Ego** (fight for what's fair) + **Coercion** (fear of losing out)
- **Content that serves him**: "What to do if you think your spouse is hiding assets", "How to respond to a Form E questionnaire"

#### Persona 3: "Michelle" - The Just-Starting
- **Who**: 30-45, just decided to divorce, doesn't know where to begin
- **Situation**: Early stages, researching everything, price-sensitive
- **Pain**: Information overload, scared of the process, doesn't know what Form E even is
- **Motivation**: **Ideology** (deserves fairness) + **Reward** (save money)
- **Content that serves her**: "What is Form E and when do you need it?", "The complete divorce timeline explained"

#### Persona 4: "David" - The DIY Determined
- **Who**: Any age, decided to self-represent, doing everything themselves
- **Situation**: Already in proceedings, actively filling out forms, hitting roadblocks
- **Pain**: Stuck on specific Form E questions, needs practical help NOW
- **Motivation**: **Ego** (prove he can do this) + **Reward** (save maximum money)
- **Content that serves him**: "Form E Section 2.20 explained", "How to fill out the income section"

- Day 1: Maybe 10 impressions

- Day 60: Some posts hitting, 500-2,000 impressions

After 1 year of daily posting: Potentially 5,000-10,000 engaged followers.

That's a distribution channel worth £100k+ in paid advertising.
