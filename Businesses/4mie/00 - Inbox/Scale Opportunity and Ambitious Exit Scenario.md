# Scale Opportunity and Ambitious Exit Scenario

## The Conservative vs Ambitious Framing

The base financial model (£1.3M Y5 revenue, £3-4M exit) assumes:
- UK only (England & Wales)
- Form E + ES2 only
- 7% of LIP market
- One-time purchases only

**That's a lifestyle business, not a scalable one.**

This note explores what's actually possible.

---

## Where's the Real Scale?

### 1. Geographic Expansion

Form E is an England & Wales document, but every common law jurisdiction has an equivalent financial disclosure requirement for divorce.

| Market | Form Equivalent | Est. LIP Volume/Year |
|--------|-----------------|----------------------|
| England & Wales | Form E | 55,000 |
| Scotland | Form F9 | ~8,000 |
| Ireland | Affidavit of Means | ~10,000 |
| Australia | Financial Statement | ~50,000 |
| Canada (Ontario) | Form 13 | ~30,000 |
| New Zealand | Financial Statement | ~8,000 |
| **Total Addressable** | | **~160,000+** |

**The insight**: Same problem, same solution, different forms. The AI + UX is transferable. Localisation is primarily form structure and legal terminology.

### 2. Product Expansion

Form E is ONE document in the divorce journey. LIPs need help with the entire process.

## Decision Framework

**Choose the lifestyle path if:**
- You want work-life balance
- Don't want to manage a team
- Happy with £3-5M outcome
- Want to stay solo

**Choose the scale path if:**
- You want a larger outcome (£30M+)
- Willing to raise capital
- Willing to build/manage a team
- See this as your "big bet"

**The good news**: You don't have to decide now. Build the Form E MVP, prove product-market fit, then choose. The MVP is the same either way.

## Key Insight

Form E is the **wedge**.

The real opportunity is **owning the LIP divorce stack** — becoming the default platform for the 80%+ of divorcing people who can't afford full solicitor representation.

That's not a £4M exit. That's a £40M+ exit.
