# Practice Direction 27A - Bundle Presentation

## Overview

Practice Direction 27A governs how court bundles must be prepared and presented in family proceedings in England and Wales. This is directly relevant to 4mie's evidence bundle generation feature.

## Key Requirements

### Bundle Contents
- Paginated continuously throughout (bottom right corner)
- Index at the front listing each document with page numbers
- Documents in chronological order within sections
- No duplicates
- Only documents that are necessary and relevant

### Bundle Structure (Typical Order)
1. Applications and orders
2. Statements and affidavits (chronological)
3. Expert reports
4. Correspondence (chronological)
5. Other documents

### Formatting
- A4 paper, single-sided preferred by some courts
- Legible copies (not photocopies of photocopies)
- Highlighting and annotation permitted but must be neutral
- Tabs/dividers between sections

### Form E Specific
- Form E typically in Section 2 after applications/orders
- Supporting financial documents (bank statements, valuations) follow Form E
- 12 months of statements required for each account
