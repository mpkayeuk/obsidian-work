## Break-even Analysis
- Average revenue per user: £310 (Pro £249 + ~20% adding Solicitor Review £299)
- Estimated cost per form (AI + infra): £5
- Solicitor payout (for ~20% adding review): ~£35/user avg across all users
- Gross margin: ~87%
- **Break-even: ~18 Pro users/month**
