## Break-even Analysis
- Average revenue per user: £410-450 (mix of Basic £99, Pro £399, Pro+Solicitor £698)
- Estimated cost per form (AI + infra): £15-20
- Solicitor payout (for ~30% adding review): ~£52/user avg across all users
- Gross margin: ~65%
- **Break-even: ~15 Pro users/month** (at £399 avg)
