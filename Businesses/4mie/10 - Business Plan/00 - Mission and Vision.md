# Mission and Vision

## Mission

To guide individuals through the financial settlement process of divorce with AI-powered tools that ensure **credibility, consistency, and protection**. We simplify the Form E process not just by filling forms, but by forensically auditing them to prevent costly legal attacks.

## Vision

To create a world where divorce financial settlements are equitable and transparent, empowering individuals to face the legal system with documents that are as robust and scrutiny-resistant as those prepared by top-tier firms.

## Core Philosophy: The "Audit Shield"
We do not just "fill forms." We actively protect the user from their own mistakes.
- **Credibility is Currency:** A messy Form E invites aggression. A perfect one commands respect.
- **Red Team First:** We act as the opposing solicitor *before* the real one does, finding gaps and contradictions so the user can fix them privately.
- **Protection over Speed:** It is better to delay a download by 10 minutes to fix a math error than to spend 3 months fighting a "Schedule of Deficiencies."
