# Subscription Tiers

This document outlines the pricing and features for CIOJoy's "unlimited service" subscription tiers. The model is based on an unlimited request queue, with tiers being differentiated by the number of requests that are worked on concurrently.

### Tier 1: Essentials

*   **Focus:** Unlimited strategic advice and governance support.
*   **Price:** £4,000 / month
*   **Structure:**
    *   **Unlimited Requests**, one at a time.
    *   Standard turnaround time (e.g., 3-5 business days per request).
    *   Includes one monthly strategic call.

### Tier 2: Growth

*   **Focus:** A faster-moving, more integrated partnership for companies that need to accelerate.
*   **Price:** £8,000 / month
*   **Structure:**
    *   **Unlimited Requests**, **two at a time**.
    *   Priority turnaround time (e.g., 1-3 business days per request).
    *   Includes a bi-weekly strategic workshop and a dedicated Slack channel.

### Tier 3: Transformation

*   **Focus:** An embedded, high-speed transformation engine.
*   **Price:** £16,000+ / month
*   **Structure:**
    *   **Unlimited Requests**, **three at a time**.
    *   Same-day response and fastest possible turnaround.
    *   Includes weekly deep-dive strategy sessions and direct leadership of major programs.
