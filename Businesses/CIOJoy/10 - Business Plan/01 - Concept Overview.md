# Concept Overview

CIOJoy is a productised consultancy that offers fractional and interim CIO services in a structured, subscription-style model. It provides organisations—especially SMEs and scale-ups—with access to enterprise-grade technology leadership without the cost or commitment of a full-time executive. The service blends strategic CIO advisory with hands-on automation and infrastructure delivery, giving clients both direction and execution capacity.

# Core Idea

CIOJoy turns the traditional consulting engagement into a repeatable, outcome-based product, combining:
•Strategic CIO leadership (governance, IT strategy, technology roadmap)
•Operational excellence (DevOps, SRE, infrastructure modernisation)
•Automation frameworks and observability tooling
•Continuous improvement via measurable KPIs and executive reporting
