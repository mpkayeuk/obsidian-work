# Elevator Pitches

## General Fractional CIO Pitch

> "CIOJoy provides expert CIO services on a flexible monthly subscription. We help founders clear 'developer fog' to ship products faster, and we help professional firms increase profitability by automating admin tasks. You get the expertise of a full-time CIO, without the long-term commitment, delivered through a system you can scale or pause as your needs change."
