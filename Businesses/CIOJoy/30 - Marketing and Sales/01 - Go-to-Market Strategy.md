# Go-to-Market Strategy

This document outlines the initial go-to-market strategies for our primary customer personas.

---

## Strategy for "Sarah, the Startup CEO"

Sarah is digitally native and lives in online communities. The strategy is to build credibility and generate inbound interest through expertise.

*   **1. Content Marketing (Build Authority):**
    *   **Action:** Write targeted blog posts and LinkedIn articles addressing her primary pain point, "Developer Fog."
    *   **Topics:** "5 Signs Your Dev Team is Drowning in 'Developer Fog'," "A Non-Technical Founder's Guide to CI/CD," "How to Structure Your Tech Team for a Seed Round."
    *   **Goal:** Demonstrate that you understand her specific problem better than anyone else.

*   **2. Community Engagement (Build Trust):**
    *   **Action:** Actively participate in founder communities where Sarah spends her time (e.g., Indie Hackers, specific subreddits like r/startups, and niche Slack groups).
    *   **Tactics:** Don't sell. Answer questions about technology strategy, scaling, and hiring. Become a trusted, helpful voice. The goal is for founders to start seeking you out.

*   **3. Targeted LinkedIn Outreach (Generate Leads):**
    *   **Action:** Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator to identify founders who fit Sarah's profile (e.g., company size 5-30, recently announced funding).
    *   **Message:** Send a hyper-personalized, concise message that speaks to her specific pain point.

## Strategy for "David, the Managing Partner"

David is a busy professional who operates on trust and referrals. The strategy is to leverage professional networks and establish high-level credibility.

*   **1. Referral Network (The Primary Channel):**
    *   **Action:** Identify and build relationships with other B2B professionals who serve David's clients but don't compete.
    *   **Targets:** Accountants, business lawyers, management consultants, and commercial insurance brokers.
    *   **Goal:** Create a formal or informal referral network for warm introductions.

*   **2. LinkedIn Leadership (Build Credibility):**
    *   **Action:** Post content focused on the business outcomes David cares about: profitability, efficiency, and risk.
    *   **Topics:** "How to Reclaim 10 Billable Hours a Week in Your Law Firm," "The 3 Systems Every Modern Accounting Firm Needs," "Is Your Client Data a Ticking Time Bomb?"
    *   **Goal:** Position yourself as a thought leader on the business of professional services, not just as a technologist.

*   **3. Speaking Engagements (Establish Expertise):**
    *   **Action:** Offer to give talks or webinars for the professional associations David belongs to (e.g., The Law Society, local Chamber of Commerce).
    *   **Tactics:** The topic should be business-focused, not technical. This is a highly effective way to generate qualified inbound leads.
