# Target Market

This document outlines the ideal customer profiles for each of CIOJoy's subscription tiers.

## Tier 1: Essentials (£4,000/month)

This tier is for organizations that need strategic technology guidance and a system for execution, but are not yet ready for a large-scale transformation.

**Profile 1: The Fast-Growing Tech Startup**
*   **Characteristics:** Pre-Series A or recently funded. 5-30 employees. The founding team is focused on product and sales, and technology is managed reactively.
*   **Primary Pain Point / Trigger:** **Lack of Speed & Progress.** They are moving fast, but their development process is chaotic. They are hitting roadblocks related to infrastructure, security, and technical debt that are slowing down their time to market. They need to build a solid foundation to scale without breaking things.

**Profile 2: The Established Professional Services Firm**
*   **Characteristics:** 10-50 employees (e.g., law firm, accounting firm, marketing agency). They have been in business for several years and rely on a collection of legacy software and systems.
*   **Primary Pain Point / Trigger:** **Crippling Technical Debt.** Their systems are outdated, inefficient, and poorly integrated. This is causing operational friction, limiting their ability to innovate, and creating security risks. They know they need to modernize but don't know where to start.

## Tier 2: Growth (£8,000/month)

This tier is for more mature businesses at a critical inflection point, where technology becomes a primary enabler of their next phase of growth.

**Profile: The Scaling Business**
*   **Characteristics:** 50-250 employees. Has found product-market fit and is now focused on scaling its operations, team, and market presence. They may have a small internal IT team, but lack senior strategic leadership.
*   **Primary Pain Point / Trigger:** **Preparing for a Major Growth Event.**
    *   **Funding:** They are preparing for a Series B or later funding round and need to demonstrate a scalable and secure technology platform to investors.
    *   **Expansion:** They are planning a major product launch or international expansion, and their current infrastructure won't support the increased load or complexity.
    *   **Scalability:** They are experiencing reliability issues, performance bottlenecks, or security concerns as their user base grows. They need to re-architect their core platform to handle 10x scale.

## Tier 3: Transformation (£16,000+/month)

This tier is for larger or more complex organizations that require deep, embedded leadership to navigate a significant period of change or crisis.

**Profile 1: The Enterprise Division**
*   **Characteristics:** A division or business unit within a larger enterprise that is underperforming or needs to modernize, but is struggling with the parent company's bureaucracy.
*   **Primary Pain Point / Trigger:** **Need for Agile Transformation.** They need to adopt more agile ways of working, modernize their technology stack, and operate like a startup, but lack the leadership or mandate to do so.

**Profile 2: The M&A / Exit-Prep Scenario**
*   **Characteristics:** A company preparing for an acquisition, or one that has recently been acquired / acquired another company.
*   **Primary Pain Point / Trigger:**
    *   **Pre-Acquisition (Sell-Side):** **Maximizing Valuation & Due Diligence Readiness.** They need to professionalize their technology, governance, and security to pass rigorous technical due diligence and command a higher valuation. This often involves a push for certifications like ISO 27001.
    *   **Post-Acquisition (Buy-Side):** **Post-Merger Integration.** They are facing the immense challenge of integrating two different technology stacks, teams, and cultures. They need a leader to manage the complexity, mitigate risks, and create a unified technology strategy.

**Profile 3: The Crisis Turnaround**
*   **Characteristics:** A business of any size that is facing a "bet-the-company" technology crisis.
*   **Primary Pain Point / Trigger:** **Critical Technology Failure.** This could be a major security breach, a catastrophic system failure, the departure of a key technical leader, or a failed digital transformation project. They need an experienced leader to step in, stabilize the situation, and create a path forward.
