# Repeatable Playbooks

This document outlines the core, repeatable processes that form the foundation of the CIOJoy service.

---

### 1. The 90-Day Tech Roadmap

*   **For:** Both Sarah and David.
*   **Problem:** They lack a clear, strategic plan for their technology that aligns with their business goals.
*   **Outcome:** A clear, one-page technology roadmap for the next 90 days, with prioritized initiatives and success metrics.
*   **Key Steps:**
    1.  **Discovery Workshop:** A session to map business goals to technology needs.
    2.  **Opportunity Mapping:** Identify and prioritize initiatives based on impact vs. effort.
    3.  **Roadmap Creation:** Distill the top 3-5 initiatives into a visual roadmap.
    4.  **Backlog Population:** Load the new initiatives into the client's request backlog.

---

### 2. The Startup CI/CD Launchpad

*   **For:** Sarah, the Startup CEO.
*   **Problem:** Her developers are stuck in "Developer Fog," with no automated way to test and release software, which slows down growth.
*   **Outcome:** A functioning CI/CD (Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment) pipeline that allows her team to ship features faster and more reliably.
*   **Key Steps:**
    1.  **Tech Stack Review:** Analyse the current application and infrastructure.
    2.  **Tool Selection & Setup:** Choose and configure the right CI/CD tools for their stack.
    3.  **Pipeline Implementation:** Build the automated workflow for testing and deploying code.
    4.  **Team Training:** A hands-on session to get the development team up to speed.

---

### 3. The Billable Hour Rescue

*   **For:** David, the Managing Partner.
*   **Problem:** His expert team is wasting valuable, billable time on manual, repetitive administrative tasks.
*   **Outcome:** The automation of one key administrative process (e.g., client onboarding), saving a significant number of hours per week and directly boosting profitability.
*   **Key Steps:**
    1.  **Process Mining Workshop:** A session with David's team to identify the most time-consuming, high-value process to automate.
    2.  **Automation Blueprint:** Design a new, streamlined workflow using modern tools (like Zapier or a dedicated practice management system).
    3.  **Implementation Guidance:** Guide the team through the implementation of the new, automated process.
    4.  **ROI Report:** Deliver a simple report showing the time saved and the direct impact on the firm's bottom line.

---

### 4. The Startup Security Health Check

*   **For:** Both Sarah and David.
*   **Problem:** They are worried about cybersecurity risks but have no idea where to start.
*   **Outcome:** A clear, jargon-free report on their top 5 security vulnerabilities, with a prioritized action plan to fix them.
*   **Key Steps:**
    1.  **Automated Scanning:** Run non-invasive scans on their public-facing assets.
    2.  **Policy Review:** Use a questionnaire to assess their internal data handling and security policies.
    3.  **Risk Analysis:** Prioritize the identified risks based on potential business impact.
    4.  **Action Plan Delivery:** Present the findings and load the remediation tasks into their request backlog.
