# Service Offerings Overview

CIOJoy's service is delivered through an "unlimited service" model, where clients can make unlimited requests to a dedicated backlog. Our team works through these requests based on the client's subscription tier.

## The "Unlimited" Model

*   **Unlimited Request Queue:** Clients can add as many requests as they like to their backlog.
*   **Concurrent Requests:** Tiers determine how many requests are worked on at the same time (1, 2, or 3).
*   **Defined Requests:** A request is a small, well-defined task. Larger projects are broken down into a sequence of requests.
*   **Asynchronous-First:** The service is delivered primarily via a project management tool and asynchronous communication, with scheduled strategic meetings.

## Fractional CIO Tiers

### Essentials Tier

*   **Concurrency:** One active request at a time.
*   **Includes:** Standard turnaround, one monthly strategy call.
*   **Best for:** SMEs and startups needing ongoing strategic guidance and governance.

### Growth Tier

*   **Concurrency:** Two active requests at a time.
*   **Includes:** Priority turnaround, bi-weekly workshops, dedicated Slack channel.
*   **Best for:** Scale-ups and businesses actively modernizing their technology stack.

### Transformation Tier

*   **Concurrency:** Three active requests at a time.
*   **Includes:** Same-day response, weekly deep-dive sessions, direct program leadership.
*   **Best for:** Organizations undergoing significant transformation or needing interim leadership.

*   **Structure:** A premium, 6-month program for pre-seed/seed stage non-technical founders.
*   **Goal:** To get from idea to a fundable, scalable MVP with an initial engineering team in place.
*   **Includes:** Operates on the "Growth" tier (2 concurrent requests) but is focused on a specific set of deliverables including technical vision, MVP management, and hiring.
*   **Best for:** Non-technical founders who need a trusted partner to build their product and team from scratch.
