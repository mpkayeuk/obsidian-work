# Service Offering: Technical Co-Founder as a Service

This document outlines the structure, deliverables, and pricing for the "Technical Co-Founder as a Service" offering.

---

### Overview

This is a premium, fixed-term program designed for pre-seed and seed-stage non-technical founders who need a trusted partner to transform their idea into a fundable, scalable Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

We act as your interim technical co-founder, providing the vision, leadership, and hands-on execution needed to build your product and your team correctly from day one.

### Key Outcomes

*   A clear and compelling technical vision and roadmap.
*   A well-architected, scalable MVP ready for launch.
*   A high-performing initial engineering team (1-2 engineers).
*   A successful handover to a permanent technical leader.

### Program Structure (6-Month Engagement)

This service operates on our "Growth" tier model (two concurrent requests) but is focused on a specific program of deliverables.

*   **Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1)**
    *   **Deliverables:** Technology Strategy & Roadmap, Tech Stack Architecture, Development Process & CI/CD Setup, Investor Pitch Deck support.

*   **Phase 2: Team & MVP (Months 2-4)**
    *   **Deliverables:** Lead the recruitment and hiring of 1-2 founding engineers, manage the MVP development process, act as the technical voice in investor meetings.

*   **Phase 3: Handover (Months 5-6)**
    *   **Deliverables:** Lead the search for a permanent CTO or Head of Engineering, manage the onboarding process, and ensure a smooth handover.

### Pricing

*   **Structure:** 6-Month minimum engagement.
*   **Price:** **£10,000 / month**
    *   *Value Proposition:* De-risk your startup and get to market faster with elite technical leadership for a fraction of the cost and equity dilution of a traditional technical co-founder.
