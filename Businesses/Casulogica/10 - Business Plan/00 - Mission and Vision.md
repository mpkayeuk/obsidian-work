# Mission and Vision

## Mission

To empower parents navigating the child arrangements order process with an intuitive app that helps them document events, organise evidence, and build a comprehensive court bundle, bringing clarity and structure to a challenging time.

## Vision

To create a world where the child arrangements process is less stressful and more organised, enabling parents to focus on the well-being of their children by providing them with the tools to present their case effectively and efficiently.
