# Daniel Priestley - Sales Strategy

Source: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE)

This document outlines Daniel Priestley's comprehensive sales methodology, designed to create a consistent and scalable sales process for any business.

## The LAPS Framework ([00:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=18s))

The core of the methodology is the LAPS framework, which stands for **Leads, Appointments, Presentations, and Sales**. It represents the engine room of your business, turning attention and interest into revenue.

---

### 1. Leads: Generating Warm and Qualified Opportunities ([01:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=87s))

The first step is to build a pipeline of warm leads. This is achieved through a three-part system:

*   **Short-Form Content (Get Noticed) ([02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=122s)):**
    *   **Goal:** To make people notice you. Research suggests someone needs to see you ~11 times within 90 days to notice you.
    *   **Frequency:** Post daily on at least one platform.
    *   **Content:** Hooks the attention of your ideal customer. Can be strange, desirable, valuable, or familiar.
    *   **Format:** 1-3 minute experiences (posts, images, short videos, carousels).
    *   **Call to Action (CTA):** Drive people to your long-form content.

*   **Long-Form Content (Build Trust) ([03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=202s)):**
    *   **Goal:** To build trust by spending time with your audience. People need 2-7 hours with you to feel like they know and trust you.
    *   **Format:** 10 minutes to 2 hours to consume (long posts, PDFs, 10-min videos, podcasts, workshops).
    *   **Strategy:** Have at least four pieces of long-form content available.

*   **Signals of Interest (Capture Leads) ([04:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=265s)):**
    *   **Goal:** To get a clear signal that someone is interested in working with you.
    *   **Mechanism:** Drive traffic from social media to a landing page on your own domain.
    *   **Examples:**
        *   Join a waiting list.
        *   Fill out an expression of interest form.
        *   Take an online assessment (e.g., using ScoreApp).
        *   Join a discussion group.
        *   Register for a live webinar.

*   **Ramping Up:**
    *   Boost high-performing short-form content with ads.
    *   Use direct messages to target specific individuals.
    *   Collaborate with others through joint ventures or sponsorships.

### 2. Appointments: Securing Time with Leads ([06:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=368s))

Once a lead is generated, the goal is to book an appointment—a commitment of time, not money.

*   **Follow-up Speed:** Follow up as quickly as possible.
*   **The Pitch Framework (Name, Same, Fame, Pain, Aim, Game) ([07:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=456s)):**
    *   **Name:** Your name and business name.
    *   **Same:** Compare yourself to something the prospect already understands (e.g., "We're like X, but for Y").
    *   **Fame:** What makes you special or different (e.g., awards, number of customers, years of experience).
    *   **Pain:** Address the frustration or problem your prospect experiences.
    *   **Aim:** What you aim to do about that pain.
    *   **Game:** The bigger vision or mission of your business.
*   **The Ask:** After the pitch, ask for the appointment and provide a link to your calendar.
*   **Lead Qualification (Advanced):** As you scale, add qualifying questions to your lead capture forms to prioritize high-quality leads.

### 3. Presentations: The 10-Step Sales Meeting ([11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=701s))

This is where you make your money. A great presentation can be the difference between a fast-growth business and one that stalls.

1.  **Framing ([12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=741s)):** Show up as high-value before you even speak. This includes your environment (physical or virtual), your title, and your overall professionalism.
2.  **Rapport ([13:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=817s)):** Build a friendly connection quickly. Use their name, find common interests, smile, and be polite.
3.  **Permission to Process ([14:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=844s)):** Ask for permission to guide the conversation. "We can either have a chitchat, or I can take you through a process to see if we're a good fit. Which do you prefer?"
4.  **Present Situation ([14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=878s)):** Understand their current situation and what's less than perfect about it.
5.  **Prize (Desired Outcome) ([15:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=902s)):** Understand the "movie in their mind." What is the ultimate outcome they want to achieve by using your product/service?
6.  **Problem ([15:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=959s)):** What obstacles, criteria, and problems are standing in their way of achieving the prize?
7.  **Insights ([17:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1045s)):** Share a big-picture perspective or framework that helps them understand their situation better. This has nothing to do with your product and establishes your authority.
8.  **Method ([18:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1135s)):** Share your proprietary methodology or process for solving their problem. This naturally follows the insight.
9.  **Solution (Gold, Silver, Bronze) ([21:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1260s)):** Present your offer, ideally with **Gold, Silver, and Bronze** options. **Crucially, use visual aids** (a brochure, slide deck, etc.). People are unlikely to buy what they can't see.
10. **Discussion & Completing ([22:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1354s)):**
    *   **Invite Discussion:** Ask open-ended questions like, "How does that land for you?"
    *   **Avoid "Conversational Tennis":** Don't get into a back-and-forth of question-answer. Collect all their questions and objections first ("Is there anything else?"), then address them together.
    *   **Complete the Sale:** If they're ready, complete the paperwork and onboard them. If they need time, schedule the next concrete step (e.g., a follow-up meeting in the diary).

### 4. Sales: Follow-up and Nurture ([26:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1584s))

You can double your business by improving your sales follow-up process.

*   **The First 48 Hours:** This is a peak time for a prospect to commit.
*   **Sales Follow-up (7-Touch Rule):**
    *   Follow up at least 7 times in the first few weeks/months.
    *   **Reasons to follow up:** Special offers, new insights, customer testimonials, or reasons that came up during the presentation.
*   **Nurture Sequence ([28:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1718s)):**
    *   If they don't convert, move them to a nurture sequence.
    *   **Content:** Friendly touchpoints like social media, emails with testimonials, insights, special offers, or commentary on the news.
*   **Reactivation Campaign ([29:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1760s)):**
    *   For dormant leads, send a "Have you given up on X?" email.
    *   If they are still interested, re-engage them with an assessment or calculator to bring them back into the LAPS process as a new lead.

## Final Tips ([30:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1835s))

*   **Use AI ([30:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1840s)):** Record and transcribe your sales presentations. Use AI (like ChatGPT) to analyse them, build a better "golf bag" of insights and methods, and identify patterns in successful vs. unsuccessful meetings.
*   **Speed Loves Money ([31:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1873s)):** The faster you follow up with a lead, the more likely you are to make the sale. Don't "play it cool."
*   **Confidence Through Consistency ([32:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE&t=1923s)):** Get into a consistent weekly LAPS rhythm. Track your numbers on a dashboard (e.g., 50 leads -> 10 appointments -> 6 presentations -> 2 sales). Consistency builds confidence and a predictable, scalable business.
